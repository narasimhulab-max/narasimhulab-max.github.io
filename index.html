About me

Biometrics, generative AI and medical imaging

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science & Engineering – Artificial Intelligence (CSE-AI) at Vasireddy Venkatadri International Technological University (VVITU), Guntur, and have taught for more than sixteen years.

I completed my Ph.D. at Vignan's Foundation for Science, Technology and Research (VFSTR) under the guidance of Dr. Susanta Kumar Satpathy. My thesis was on deep learning-based heterogeneous face recognition: matching a person's identity across very different image types, such as forensic sketches and photographs.

My current work uses diffusion models and multimodal transformers to synthesise and recognise faces across domains. I apply the same ideas to trustworthy, explainable AI for early breast cancer diagnosis.

Research

Research interests

Building AI that is accurate, explainable and useful in security and healthcare.

Heterogeneous Face Recognition

Matching identities across sketches, photographs and other imaging modalities for forensic and security applications.

Generative AI for Biometrics

Photo–sketch synthesis and identity-preserving diffusion models with controllable conditioning.

Deepfake Detection

Detecting synthetic and manipulated facial media to protect trust in digital identity.

Medical Image Analysis

Explainable, uncertainty-aware models for early breast cancer detection from ultrasound and mammography.

Projects

Current research projects

Ongoing work with collaborators and students.

ARDiff-HFS

Manuscript ready

Sketch-to-photo synthesis for heterogeneous face recognition. A diffusion model is conditioned on raw sketches through ControlNet, and an agentic best-of-N step chooses the candidate that best preserves the person's identity.

Diffusion modelsControlNetFace recognition

ACMFT: Adaptive Cross-Modal Foundation Transformer

In progress

A multimodal transformer that adaptively fuses information across modalities. It serves as a foundation-style backbone for cross-domain recognition.

Multimodal learningTransformersFoundation models

Explainable AI for Breast Cancer Diagnosis

In progress

Uncertainty-aware deep networks on breast ultrasound, using Monte-Carlo dropout and Grad-CAM explanations. The work is being extended into a multimodal mixture-of-experts model that combines mammography, ultrasound and clinical data.
